2. Publicly Available Datasets Used

  • Dataset 1 — Taiwanese Credit Card Default Dataset (UCI ML Repository): https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/350/default+of+credit+card+clients
    Contains 30,000 client records and 24 financial features (credit limit, demographics, 6 months of repayment status and bill statements). Imbalanced binary target: 77.88% Non-Default vs. 22.12% Default.
  • Dataset 2 — Statlog German Credit Dataset (UCI ML Repository): https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/144/statlog+german+credit+data
    Contains 1,000 loan applicant records and 20 financial/demographic features. Imbalanced binary target: 70.0% Good Credit Risk vs. 30.0% Bad Credit Risk (Default).

3. Alignment with Course Methodology & Reported Metrics

  • Pre-Processing & Class Balancing: Feature scaling, encoding, and hybrid resampling comparing SMOTE, ENN (Edited Nearest Neighbors), and SMOTE-ENN.
  • Classification Models Taught in Course: Logistic Regression (LR), Decision Tree (DT), Support Vector Machine (SVM), Random Forest (RF), AdaBoost, XGBoost, and Multilayer Perceptron (MLP).
  • Evaluation Protocol & Baseline Metrics: 10-fold stratified cross-validation reporting Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and ROC-AUC (reporting 95.4% Accuracy / 0.928 F1 with MLP + SMOTE-ENN on German Credit, and 82.1% Accuracy / 0.789 F1 with Random Forest + SMOTE-ENN on Taiwanese Credit).

4. Reason for Selection & Planned Improvement (Step 6 & Step 7)

We selected this March 2026 study because automated credit risk underwriting is a vital real-world financial problem, its tabular datasets are fully reproducible on standard compute, and it presents a compelling scientific opportunity for Step 6 (Critical Analysis) and Step 7 (Bonus Improvement):

  • Critical Replication Audit: Hybrid resampling (SMOTE-ENN) applied prior to cross-validation splits causes boundary leakage, and altering class priors distorts predicted Probability of Default (PD) estimates required under Basel III banking standards.
  • Proposed Improvement: We will implement a strict in-fold resampling pipeline paired with Isotonic/Platt Probability Calibration (evaluated via Brier Score and Expected Calibration Error) and Instance-Dependent Cost-Sensitive Thresholding using each borrower's actual credit limit (LIMIT_BAL) to minimize total financial portfolio loss.
